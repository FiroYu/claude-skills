# limit-break · 限界突破

<img src="assets/limit-break-banner-v6.webp" alt="Black-and-white shōnen manga banner: under a looming demon lord, a fallen hero kneels at his lowest point as the skill 限界突破 ignites — one thin beam of light cutting through despair" width="896">

**English**

The path ends here. The old method has failed, and the deadline is closing in. The hero draws one breath and releases the skill sealed until this moment: LIMIT BREAK. Choose the axis of the breakthrough—speed, altitude, depth, or range. Before striking, ask what an expert would demand: which work is missing, which original materials matter, which tools fit, and what evidence could settle the outcome. Then test paths that change the mechanism, including the one that looks too bold to try. Build something real. Let the source material challenge your assumptions and let results beyond your own judgment decide whether the move worked. If it fails, learn where the mechanism broke and strike again; if the battle returns, forge the lesson into a workflow that can run the next round. No victory is declared on conviction alone. The limit moves only as far as the evidence shows.

**中文**

旧路走到了尽头，期限步步逼近。勇者握紧剑柄，解放固有技能——「限界突破」。先选定要突破的方向：速度、高度、深度，或广度；再把专家会追问的缺口、原始材料、具体工具和外部判据写进任务。不要只把旧招使得更猛，还要寻找能绕开瓶颈、重构流程的不同机制，哪怕其中一条看起来太过大胆。拿出真正的作品或实验，让原始材料推翻臆测，让真实反馈裁决成败。失败了，就查清是哪项假设或机制失效，再出下一招；若战斗会反复到来，就把验证与行动锻造成持续运转的工作流。胜利无需夸口，突破到了哪里，只由证据作答。

Method source · 方法来源: [How to be more ambitious — Felix Rieseberg](https://felixrieseberg.com/how-to-be-more-ambitious-a-tutorial-for-humans-with-models/)

#### About the method · 方法说明

**English**

Felix Rieseberg’s October 2026 article, *How to be more ambitious: A tutorial for humans with models*, argues that assumptions about what is possible constrain how people use models whose capabilities are advancing unevenly. Rieseberg, who states that he works on Claude, provides practical techniques for attempting previously unconsidered outcomes.

The framework distinguishes Speed (the same work faster), Altitude (outcomes beyond individual tasks), Depth (expert methods), and Range (cross-disciplinary approaches). Speed is established and left aside. Altitude questions artifacts and seeks durable workflows with autonomous data access and verification. Depth requires professional instruments, unreasonable goals stated as reasonable, adversarial reproduction of assumptions from raw data, new knowledge validated on unseen data, and an outside judge. Range replaces nouns with verbs to identify relevant disciplines and assigns concrete investigative work rather than roleplay.

`limit-break` selects a primary axis, defines the outcome and success evidence, and establishes a conventional baseline, the user’s goal, and a challenge goal, marking unknown baselines as assumptions. It specifies expert work, raw materials, methods, tools, and independent validation before exploring distinct approaches, including an unusually bold path. It then tests interpretations against raw evidence and counterexamples.

A prototype or experiment tests the decisive assumption through external feedback, with predefined controls, metrics, pass/fail criteria, and a timebox. Iteration targets failed assumptions or mechanisms. Recurring goals become workflows validated against history and piloted alongside existing processes. Delivery prioritizes usable results and separates verified, inferred, simulated, and untested findings; failure reports include eliminated paths and the next informative test. Comparison levels, budgets, and evidence-state rules are implementation additions.

**中文**

Felix Rieseberg 于 2026 年 10 月发表的文章《How to be more ambitious: A tutorial for humans with models》指出，模型在不同方向上的能力发展并不均衡，而人们对“什么是可能的”预设限制了模型的使用方式。作者在文中说明自己参与 Claude 的研发，并提供了一组具体方法，帮助读者尝试此前未曾认真考虑的目标。

文章将雄心分为四个维度：Speed（更快完成相同工作）、Altitude（追求单项任务背后的结果）、Depth（采用专家级方法）和 Range（引入其他领域的方法）。文章认为 Speed 已较为成熟，因此未展开讨论。Altitude 要求审视交付物本身是否合适，并寻求能自主获取数据、验证结果的持久工作流。Depth 要求使用专业工具，将看似不合理的目标作为合理要求提出，从原始数据中对抗性地复现既有判断，在未见数据上验证新发现，并由外部评判结果。Range 通过将名词改写为动词来识别相关学科，再安排具体的调查研究工作，而非进行角色扮演。

`limit-break` 首先选择一个主要突破维度，明确目标结果及其成功证据，再设定常规基线、用户目标和挑战目标；未知基线须标明为假设。随后，它先写明专家会开展的工作、所需原始材料、具体方法与工具，以及独立验证方式，再探索实质不同的路径，其中包括至少一条看似过于大胆的方案。接着，它用原始证据和反例检验已有解释。

执行阶段通过原型或实验检验决定性假设，预先确定对照、指标、通过与失败标准及时间限制，并获取外部反馈。迭代围绕失效的假设或机制展开。对于反复出现的目标，技能将其转化为持久工作流，用历史记录验证，并与现有流程并行试运行。交付优先提供可用结果，区分已验证、推断、模拟和未测试的发现；失败时说明已排除的路径及下一项最有信息价值的测试。对比层级、预算和证据状态规则属于技能实现层面的补充。
